## Current claim scope

- Exact two- and four-dimensional native endpoints: accepted modern source, kernel and raw endpoint audits, with one explicit parent-occurrence premise C in each aperiodicity conclusion. Compactness, nonempty interior and physical tiling existence are C-free.
- Dimension three and every dimension at least five: unconditional physical monotile existence in a retained historical verification. The modern port and the uniform all-dimension assembly remain pending.
- Four-dimensional geometry: 56 pyramidal keys and a source-level geometric/exact-arithmetic count of 362 maximal connected flat three-dimensional boundary patches. The patches lie in 346 distinct containing affine hyperplanes. This is not a count of two-dimensional ridges or a completed body-level Lean count theorem. The separate exact-body polyhedrality formalization is unfinished.
- Named prior bodies: the exact sparse CLARK tile has a completed five-dimensional Lean formalization and Palomar registration. The sparse seven-dimensional HENRY body has a written computer-assisted proof; its complete Lean verification is unfinished. The dense seeds used in product closure are not identified with those sparse bodies.

The all-dimension contract selects compactness, nonempty interior, physical tiling existence and absence of nonzero translation periods under arbitrary Euclidean isometries. It does not assert uniqueness, ball topology, polyhedrality in every dimension or a full-symmetry bound.
