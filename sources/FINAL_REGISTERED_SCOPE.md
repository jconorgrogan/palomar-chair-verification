# Final registered-family theorem and boundaries

## No initial-world or closure premise

For every concrete `P : Parameters`, `registered_proper_family P` constructs:

- A full `RegisteredWorld P.p` with actual generated-E legality and proved proper frames
- An `AtlasHierarchy P W` with full registered worlds and exact marked subdivision reconstruction
- Proper frames at every hierarchy level
- At every level, zero as the only integer translation period preserving the full marked tile set

For every ordinary odd prime given by `2≤p`, the elementary prime-divisor condition and `p%2=1`, `every_odd_prime_registered_proper_family` constructs such a parameter and family with μ=1. The separate all-Parameters theorem covers every nonzero square μ and nonzero nonsquare g.

Finally `registered_proper_family_dimensions_unbounded N` has only the natural bound N as input. It constructs P.p>N together with the proper world, hierarchy and all-level marked aperiodicity. Thus infinitude is not left as an external prime-existence fact.

## Definitions and proved assumptions

Prime, square and nonsquare mean ordinary Nat divisor and remainder predicates. Coordinate arithmetic uses concrete Nat/Int residues. Child properness, field cancellation/inverses, mean covariance, Euler/Zolotarev and all atlas invariants are proved from those data.

A `RegisteredWorld` has full integer-cell coverage and full-pose nonoverlap. Those fields are hypotheses when discussing an arbitrary input tiling, and are explicitly proved for the constructed existence witness. No initial world is supplied to the final family theorem.

`GeneratedContact` is a genuine face contact between actual finite descendants of the arithmetic rule, with exact full relative pose equality. It is not a proposed symbolic catalog.

## Scope retained

The formal period vector is `Fin p → Int`, and the period preserves full marked poses. A Real-coordinate realization theorem and transfer to unframed physical tile collections are not exported here.

Initial worlds are actual-E legal. Higher worlds satisfy the proved closed arithmetic atlas. E=atlas and actual-E legality at every higher level are not claimed; the stronger ordinary-paper language theorem remains distinct.

Arbitrary physical placement, dense matching-profile realization, sparse S54 registration, and physical T5/T7 endpoints remain separate developments. No transfer between them is inferred.
