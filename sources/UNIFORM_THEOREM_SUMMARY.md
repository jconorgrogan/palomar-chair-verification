## Exact family

Let p be an odd prime. Coordinates are indexed by F_p. For each nonempty proper support A, k=|A| and z_A=(sum A)/k, choose the outer frame with index permutation

    f_A(i)=μ g^k(i−z_A)

and signs (−1)^1_A, at translation 4·1_A. Here μ is any nonzero square and g any nonsquare. The centre has permutation μi, positive signs, and translation 1.

ADDITIONAL ENDPOINT ASSUMPTION: the empty outer role also has permutation μi. This completes the undefined zero-popcount quotient. No all-ones outer child exists. None of these results silently derives that endpoint from division by zero.

## Main theorem

For this family, every lattice-registered tiling obeying the whole-contact language E has a unique nested partition into complete substituted chairs at every level. Such tilings exist. They have no nonzero translational periods and have finite Euclidean symmetry groups. Thus the registered marked-frame model is strongly aperiodic, meaning that every admitted tiling has a finite Euclidean symmetry group.

This holds for EVERY nonsquare g. One explicit family is μ=1 and g(p) the least positive quadratic nonresidue modulo p. The proof is analytic and uniform; it does not infer the theorem from the p=3,5,7 checks.

## A precise one-shell obstruction

Define an inverse-closed one-root support operator exactly as in ONE_SHELL_SUPPORT_AND_CENSUS.md. Its fixed-point registry is

    E union {Q_a: a∈g<g²> minus {g,g^(-1)}},

where Q_a has permutation ai, negative axis {0}, and translation 4e_0. The gap is0 for d=2, and d/2−2 for d>2. Thus its gap vanishes exactly when d≤4.

At p=17 every nonsquare has order16, so six such ghosts survive this one-shell operator. This obstructs an all-odd-prime G=0 statement for this completed family and this operator. It does not obstruct the proved two-shell/global forcing theorem.

## Corona census scope

Let N=d/2 and q=p for d=2, q=p+1 for d>2. The exact number of complete E-legal one-root coronas is

    1+(N^p+1)[2^p−1+pq].

For d=2 (g=−1, hence p≡3 mod4), the complete live/dead two-shell census is

    total: 2^(p+1)−1+2p²
    dead:  2p²
    live:  2^(p+1)−1.

This gives 33→15 at p=3,353→255 at p=7, and4337→4095 at p=11. For d>2, the uniform parent-forcing theorem is proved, but an exact numerical count of all two-shell-live corona types is NOT claimed.
